S-Video

A calm, iPad-only RSS and Atom video feed player built for winding down and falling asleep.

View on GitHub Requires iPadOS 17.0 or later • Free & Open Source (MIT)

Single Chronological Timeline

Unwatched, in-progress, and recently completed videos from all of your sources in one clean, reverse-chronological list.

Universal Video Sources

Subscribe to up to 100 RSS 2.0 or Atom video feeds—video podcasts, PeerTube, Vimeo, YouTube channels, or self-hosted feeds.

Sleep Timer & Dim Screen

15, 30, 45, or 60 minute countdowns—or End of Video—with a gentle 15-second audio fade-out, plus 40% / 65% / 80% nightstand dimming.

Private iCloud Sync

Optionally sync only your video sources and watch progress across your iPads via your private iCloud database—zero duplicates, fast setup.

Playback Your Way

Resume where you left off, optionally auto-play the next video in your list, copy links at exact timecodes, or open in Safari.

Portable Backups

Export and import your subscriptions via standard OPML or full S-Video JSON backups including watch progress.
